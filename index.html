About Us

Founded in 2020 by a collective of passionate rug artists and educators, Tufting.xyz emerged from a simple vision: to make the art of rug tufting accessible to everyone. What started as small workshop sessions has grown into a comprehensive online learning platform, helping thousands of students worldwide discover their creative potential.

Our mission is to empower creators with the skills, knowledge, and confidence to bring their artistic visions to life through rug tufting. We believe in fostering a supportive community where creativity thrives, and everyone has the opportunity to transform their passion into success. Our core values of accessibility, excellence, and continuous innovation drive everything we do.
